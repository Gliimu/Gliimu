How long is the program?

The program is untimed, but expected to take about a year. You graduate when you prove competence through practical work, which can take as little as 2 months.

How much is the tuition?

We charge a premium of ₦650,000 for absolute value. We take a maximum of 9 students per Hub to deliver our absolute best.

Do I need prior experience?

No. We train elite minds from the ground up, grouping you in a Triad to ensure you learn at your own pace without holding others back.

Will I get a job after graduation?

We don't train employees; we train independent bosses. You will start earning from real paid gigs before you even graduate.
