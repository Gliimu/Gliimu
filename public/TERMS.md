## 4. Wallet Balance, GP & Consumption Model

Gliimu operates on a dual-currency system:

- **Wallet Balance (Real Money)**: Your wallet balance is denominated in Naira (₦) and represents real money. You can top up your wallet via bank transfer or receive funds from other users. Content consumption charges are deducted from your wallet balance. Wallet balance is withdrawable to bank accounts or transferable to other users on the platform.
- **GP (Gliimu Points)**: GP is earned through platform activities (posting, engagement, completing tasks). GP cannot be purchased or topped up. GP is used to unlock feature eligibility (e.g., posting privileges, premium features) and serves as a credibility metric for creators. GP is non-withdrawable and has no monetary value outside the platform.
- **Subscription Tiers**: You subscribe to a plan (Starter, Pro, or Elite) which grants platform access and may include monthly wallet credits or GP bonuses.
- **Per-Consumption Charges**: When you open content (a book, audiolite, or bundle), a charge is deducted from your wallet balance for a consumption session (approximately 3 hours of access). If you close the content and return later, a new session charge applies.
- **No Permanent Ownership**: You are renting access to content, not buying it. Content may be removed by the creator or platform at any time.
- **Refunds**: All payments are final and non-refundable. Wallet balance spent on consumption sessions is not refundable, even if content is later removed. Gliimu retains discretion to issue refunds in exceptional cases of verifiable platform failure.
